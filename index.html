Good to know

A few quick
answers.

Everything you might want to know before taking your seat.

Can I book an appointment through WhatsApp?

Absolutely. Message us on WhatsApp with your preferred service and appointment time. Our team can help you with the next available slot.

Do you accept walk-ins?

Walk-ins are welcome, subject to availability. Booking ahead is recommended, especially for busy hours and occasion styling.

How much do your services cost?

Prices vary depending on the service and styling requirements. Contact us for current pricing before your visit.

What is included in the Groom Package?

Our Groom Package is designed for complete occasion-ready styling. Message us to discuss your requirements, inclusions and package details.

Where is John Saloon located?

We are at Kakinada Main Road, Kalpana Centre, Kakinada. Use the Get directions link for navigation.
